Privacy Policy — DevTools for AI Agents

Effective date: November 5, 2026

DevTools for AI Agents (“the Extension”) is developed and supported by an individual developer. This policy describes how the Extension handles data.

What the free Extension does

You choose what to import. The Extension only processes logs you explicitly paste, drop as a file, or import via a user-initiated clipboard action. It does not silently collect browsing history, page content, or agent traffic. Built-in “Load sample” items are synthetic fixtures only. We never collect or upload your real logs unless we first prompt you and you agree.

Imported traces are processed locally in your browser and stored in IndexedDB on your device. Free forever for local debugging: nothing leaves your machine on free. Free includes unlimited local visualization, up to 50 traces of history, and basic PNG/JSON export. Removing the Extension or clearing site data may delete local history.

Logs may contain prompts, tool arguments, results, or other sensitive information. Only import data you are authorized to inspect. Local processing does not automatically redact secrets.

Permissions

The Extension requests only storage and sidePanel. It does not request host permissions or capture tabs in the current version.

Support email

Support contact: agentdevtools@proton.me, delivered through Proton Mail. Proton encrypts email in transit and at rest under Proton’s privacy policy. We retain support correspondence only as needed to reply and delete it on request. Do not email raw traces, API keys, or passwords.

Pro (not required for local use)

Pro is $9/month or $49 one-time: cost dashboards, exportable reports, team folders, priority parsers, unlimited history/search. Cloud share is planned later and will be opt-in; this policy will be updated before any cloud feature ships. Free remains local-only with no network calls.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
